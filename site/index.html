The gap

Tablets can tutor — or they can distract

Families already stitch together timers, blockers, and quiz apps so kids practice before they play. QuizGate is Clevertree’s forever-free, Android-first take: Hard Accessibility lock, local stats, closed-loop SI for curriculum packs, parents decide.

Education earns time

Android-first

Raising kids

A third choice.

When you raise a child, you choose who helps. Hold everything yourself. Hand it to someone else. Or invite a stable, objective partner you can actually freeze and override.

Hold the control

Parents do it all. Noble — and brittle. Exhausting. Often incomplete when life gets loud.

Give it to somebody else

School, daycare, apps, platforms. Third parties raise kids in ways you cannot fully see or freeze. Feeds change. Policies shift. The open internet does not wait for your consent.

Closed-loop SI

Allow a completely objective, safe SI in a closed loop — not the greater internet’s ad and identity mesh. It does not change unless we publish a new curriculum pack. You can turn sync off. You always decide.

Letting others help raise kids is good when they are good people. Clevertree’s job is to make this SI the best “person” we can — curated, stable, parent-overridable. Preference and final calls stay with humans, especially parents.

Naming

We say SI — not AI

We say SI (Super Intelligence, i.e. AI) for the software that files, summarizes, and curates. SI is the better name for what this software does: accessibility and curation at a scale a person alone cannot keep fair — not a new life-form, not a referee of truth, not a chat tutor living in your child’s cloud profile.

Batch SI for packs

Online, SI reviews curriculum packs in batch — wording, difficulty, duplicates — then humans publish signed versions. The kid app never becomes an open generative feed.

On-device learning SoT

Stats and progress stay local. Gallery photos stay local. Curriculum downloads are optional content sync, not an account.

Reward-based time

Earn the next stretch — don’t race a timer

QuizGate schedules Hard quizzes while the tablet is in use (about every 40 minutes ±10 minutes) and at morning check-in. One correct answer unlocks. Finish a round; correct answers fill a minutes jar. Sync curricula when you want — or keep the bundled basic-math pack offline forever.

Curriculum

Signed packs. Optional sync. Parent override.

Curricula are versioned packs — not a live feed. The APK lists packs by age band and difficulty. Auto-sync can be turned off. SI batch curates what authors publish; parents choose what runs on the tablet.

Progressive system

Start with early gates. As kids succeed, move to the next tier. Struggle signals stay local until you opt into aggregate review for pack authors.

SI batch suggestions

Optional batch reviews suggest “needs practice” vs “ready to advance” for curricula — never as a side-taking judge of your child. Parents decide.

Direction

We are not a recognized 501(c)(3) yet. Clevertree LLC ships QuizGate today as a forever-free educational tool. Languages & curricula

Landscape

Similar products families already use

Anything that turns practice into screen time — math unlocks, quiz gates, earn-minutes apps, or learning bolted onto parental controls — sits in the same neighborhood as QuizGate. This list is descriptive, not an endorsement. Platforms, pricing, and privacy change; check each product yourself.

Earn minutes / quiz-to-unlock (kids)

  • Pickles iOS

    Math earns minutes; Screen Time / Family Controls locking; free, no accounts, no tracking.

  • Screenwise Android

    Parental-control suite with times-tables practice to earn minutes, app blocking, schedules; Play listing shows IAP.

  • ScreenQuest Android

    Math, reading, vocabulary, puzzles Pre-K–8; practice earns time; optional app protection; subscription after trial.

  • Test 4 Time Android

    Blocks selected apps; grade-level math; each correct answer earns a minute; subscription.

  • Math Applocker Android

    Locks chosen apps behind math challenges on an interval; parental PIN; trial / paid.

  • Solve2Open Android

    Add/subtract (and similar) to unlock selected apps; Accessibility-based; free / Families Policy listing.

  • Lockee iOS

    Grades 1–5 math for points redeemed as minutes; Family Controls blocking.

  • Solve To Play iOS

    Short math rounds unlock chosen apps/categories for a set window via Screen Time.

  • Think2Unlock iOS

    Math facts (and optional vocab) every N minutes; free core; optional parent dashboard.

  • Applatch Kids iOS / Android (check store)

    Curriculum-style maths/English/science quizzes unlock entertainment apps; parent device control; sleep window locks.

  • TutorLock Mobile

    Questions across subjects before / during entertainment apps; custom parent/teacher questions.

  • LockdIn, Kids Focus, BrainKey, and similar “math locker” listings Android / iOS

    Store apps that lock games until a quiz is passed — often trials, IAP, or subscriptions. Search your store for current names.

Same mechanic, not kids-first

  • PlugBrain Android · F-Droid / Play

    Open-source math gate for distraction apps — adult / focus framing; free forever, offline.

  • Scrolliosis Android · OSS

    Custom knowledge-vault questions before scroll / unlock; self-hosted style learning gate.

Platform timers families combine with learning

  • Google Family Link Android

    OS-level limits and approvals — often paired with a separate quiz or worksheet routine.

  • Apple Screen Time / Family Controls iOS / iPadOS

    System shields and Downtime; several earn-minutes apps above build on these APIs.

  • OEM kids / Digital Wellbeing modes Samsung, etc.

    Device profiles and app timers without curriculum — still used the same way when parents add homework or flashcards first.

Practice apps parents use as the “earn” half

  • Khan Academy Kids, Prodigy, IXL, DreamBox, Mathletics, Zap Zap Math, Moose Math, Quick Math Multi-platform

    Not unlock gates themselves — families often require a round here before Family Link / Screen Time unlocks play.

  • Duolingo ABC, Epic!, ABCmouse, Homer, Reading Eggs Multi-platform

    Literacy / early-learning practice used the same “earn then play” household rule.

QuizGate’s bet: Hard Accessibility lock on Android today, signed curriculum packs, closed-loop SI for authors, forever free — without turning into an ad-funded control suite. If you know another tool that fits this list, open an issue on GitHub.
