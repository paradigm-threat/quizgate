The gap

Pickles-class education exists on iOS — not Android

The narrative we want is already clear on iPhone: math earns minutes, no kids accounts, no tracking, no fees. That product is Pickles — and it is not on Android. Tablets and family Android phones still get parental-control stacks, ads, or shallow “learning” shells.

iOS has the bar

Pickles: free, private, no sign-in — right answers earn minutes, then the games open. Same values we share. Android is where we focus.

Android is cluttered

Play peers lean toward heavy blockers, web filters, and control suites. Useful tools — not the same “just education” promise.

Undershooting potential

Tablets can be tutors. Instead they become slot machines. Learning-through-tech should gate time — not an afterthought bolted onto surveillance.

Reward-based time

Earn the next stretch — don’t race a timer

QuizGate is a soft interval quiz, not a hard app lock (yet). When the gate appears, a short challenge earns more time. Too easy? The curriculum advances.

Roadmap

Curriculum, then gentle AI curation

Progress stays anonymous by design. No accounts, photos, or PII in a learning cloud for QuizGate’s source of truth.

Progressive system

Start with early gates. As kids succeed, move to the next tier. Struggle signals stay local until you opt into batch review.

AI batch suggestions

Optional reviews can suggest “needs practice” vs “ready to advance.” Parents decide. Kid-led interests stay welcome — with math basics still gated.

CleverTree policy

Forever free. No ads. No IAP. Ever.

QuizGate — and every CleverTree product — ships without ads, in-app purchases, or paywalls. Other free apps exist in this space; our pledge is permanent company policy, not a launch promo.

Same north star as Pickles — education earns time, privacy stays local — brought to Android under CleverTree’s forever-free policy. We do not claim to be the only free app.

How we differ

Android-first. Pickles-class values.

Pickles is the product we measure against: math for screen time, no accounts, no ads, no tracking — iOS only today. On Android the closest Play listing is heavier parental-control software like Screenwise (blocker + filter + math minutes, IAP badge on the store). QuizGate stays education-first, forever free, and built for the Android gap Pickles does not fill.
