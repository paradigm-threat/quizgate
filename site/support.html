Support the mission

Help QuizGate stay free and grow carefully

The app is forever free — no ads, no purchases. We are not a recognized 501(c)(3) yet. If the MVP succeeds, a dedicated educational nonprofit — working name QuizGate Educational Foundation, Inc., separate from Clevertree LLC — would steward the QuizGate technique (learn, then unlock time) so gifts can fund curriculum, licenses, guardrails, and deployment worldwide.

Important

No tax-deductible receipts yet

Until a nonprofit is formed and the IRS issues a determination letter, do not treat payments as deductible charitable contributions. We will not issue 501(c)(3) donation receipts before that. See Mission.

Ways to help now

Feedback, code, and patience

Cash fundraising waits on the nonprofit. What helps immediately is real-world use and clear feedback.

Parents & teachers

Tell us what works, what confuses kids, and which languages or topics you need next. Email support@clevertree.net or open a GitHub issue.

Builders

Star, fork, and contribute on GitHub / GitLab. Curriculum and pedagogy notes live in the public docs.

Later: gifts to the nonprofit

When the educational nonprofit exists and may solicit donations, funds would go to research, education-oriented licenses, content guardrails, hardware/deployment, and modest access infrastructure — never to unlock a paywall.
