Why QuizGate exists

Education earns time — for every family we can reach

QuizGate turns idle tablet time into short, age-fit learning. It is forever free: no ads, no in-app purchases, no paywall. Clevertree LLC ships the MVP today. Our intent is a dedicated educational nonprofit — separate from Clevertree LLC — so that if the MVP succeeds, donations can fund the long work of making QuizGate usable worldwide.

Status (honest)

Not a recognized 501(c)(3) yet

We have not filed for IRS tax-exempt recognition. We are stating direction early so parents, partners, and future donors know the plan. Clevertree LLC remains a separate for-profit company. Commercial products such as MyProblem are not part of this educational mission.

When a Georgia nonprofit is formed and (later) recognized, gifts to that organization can become tax-deductible. Until then, do not treat support as a deductible charitable contribution unless we say otherwise in writing.

If the MVP succeeds

What donations would fund

Not a paywall for the app. Not a Relay-style “server in every place” product vision. The hard part is curriculum, law, and quality at world scale — work that takes people, research, and time, not only AI generation.

Hardware & deployment

Help put QuizGate on devices and into hands so families and partners can simply use it — install support, practical rollout, and the basics of running the program.

Access, not geo-politics

Modest regional servers or CDN where needed so curriculum and updates load reliably. This is about good access — not building a location-based network like the broader Relay vision.

Every language & curriculum

Age-fit packs across languages and subjects. Scaling that responsibly needs real research and educators. AI can assist; it cannot replace the work.

Licenses & the law

Education-oriented licensing and compliance so packs stay lawful and respectful of existing education norms.

Guardrails, continually

A worldwide process — people, not only models — so content stays education-worthy for the age band: no controversial topics spilled out of context, no material that would not be deemed appropriate for learning.

Forever free remains

Donations expand reach and quality. They do not unlock the app. No ads. No IAP. No subscription to “be educational.”
