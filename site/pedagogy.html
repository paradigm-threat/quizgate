Educational record

How QuizGate teaches

Short, age-fit practice replaces idle screen time. Learning unlocks the next stretch. Parents keep the schedule, the pack, and the final say.

Learners

Age band & basic-math objectives

Current bundled pack basic-math (v4) targets roughly ages 4–7: addition and subtraction within 10, light multiplication within 10, and zero-identity addition as its own skill — not dumped into the easiest tier.

Many ways to ask

The same fact can appear as an equation blank, true/false, or multiple choice so fluency is not tied to one layout.

Short Hard sessions

One correct answer unlocks a scheduled lock. The point is a successful burst — not a stacked multi-question jail.

Skip without shame

Skip is allowed and logged; the picker soft-biases toward easier formats afterward so frustration does not trap the child.

Time-earn

Mastery over speed-grinding

Jar minutes add free time on top of the normal ~40±10 minute window — they never shrink it. Slow-but-correct still earns a floor. Session caps stop unbounded grinding. Optional “earn more” after unlock invites voluntary practice once the lock duty is done.

Correct earns

Skips earn zero. Engagement with the fact is what banks time.

Floor for slow

Faster recall can earn more; a tired correct answer still counts — no punishment spiral.

Cap the jar session

Default 120-minute session cap (tunable) so free time cannot be farmed without limit.

Guardrails

Signed packs, not an open feed

Curriculum is versioned content parents choose — not a live generative tutor on the child’s profile. Expanding to many languages and subjects needs research, educators, licenses, and human review so material stays education-worthy and age-fit.
