Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

QuizGate is a product of Clevertree. Contact: via clevertree.net.

Summary

QuizGate is designed to be local-first. The Android app does not require an account. Learning stats stay on the device. Gallery photos used as quiz backdrops are read on-device and are not uploaded by QuizGate.

Data we process on-device

  • Quiz progress and stats (right / wrong / skipped) stored in local app storage.
  • Device photos (with permission) shown as decorative backdrops during quizzes — never sent to Clevertree servers by the current app.
  • Alarms / notifications for scheduled Hard quizzes (about every 40 minutes ±10 minutes, plus morning).
  • Usage access (optional, system toggle) so QuizGate can pause media during a lock quiz and return the child to the app they were just using — processed on-device only; not uploaded.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts or passwords for QuizGate learning features.
  • No advertising identifiers sold or used for ads (there are no ads).
  • No in-app purchases or payment data.
  • No intentional upload of photos, contacts, or child PII to Clevertree for the product’s source of truth.

Curriculum sync (optional)

If auto-sync is enabled, the app may download signed curriculum packs (questions and optional media) from Clevertree-hosted indexes. Sync can be turned off; a bundled basic-math pack works offline. This is content sync only — not a child account.

Anonymous progress sync (optional, not enabled in current Play builds)

QuizGate may later sync anonymized learning progress to Clevertree’s QuizGate data plane at api.quizgate.app. That design uses a random device UUID (capability token) — not your name, email, or advertising ID. Quiz answer outcomes, day rollups, and coarse performance signals can help SI (Super Intelligence, i.e. AI) batch jobs decide curriculum readiness and quiz frequency. Photos and audio used in realtime analysis are not stored in the permanent progress repo; the app build keeps network upload off until this section ships in production. Parents keep final decisions.

Future optional features

Additional opt-in SI features will update this policy before shipping. We minimize data and avoid using personal photos as cloud learning records of truth.

Children

QuizGate is intended for families and children with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing. Parents control device permissions (gallery, notifications, battery exemptions, usage access).

Third parties

This marketing site may be hosted on Vercel. Hosting providers may process standard request logs (IP, user agent) under their own policies. The open-source app repositories are hosted on GitHub and GitLab.

Changes

We will revise this page when product behavior changes. The “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
