FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QuizGate helps kids earn tablet time by answering short math quizzes.

Correct answers bank minutes. Soft reminders ask for a quick round on your schedule (default about every half hour, with a little randomness) plus a morning check-in. Parents stay in control — forever free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

How it works
• Soft gate: a short quiz before the next stretch of screen time
• Minutes jar: finish a round to bank time; skip early and nothing is earned
• Local stats: see today’s asked / right / wrong / skipped on the home screen
• Photos: use your family’s gallery pictures as backdrops, or kid-friendly defaults
• Optional curriculum sync: download signed packs over Wi‑Fi, or stay offline with the bundled basic-math pack

Built for families
• No child accounts required
• No ads, no IAP, no subscriptions — Clevertree forever-free policy
• Gallery photos and learning progress stay on the device
• Closed-loop SI helps curate curriculum packs; parents decide what runs

Permissions (why we ask)
• Notifications & exact alarms — soft quiz reminders on your schedule
• Photos — optional quiz backdrops from this device
• Usage access (optional) — pause media during a quiz and return to the app your child was just using
• Battery unrestricted — so reminders stay reliable on some devices

QuizGate is open source. Privacy policy: https://quizgate.app/privacy
