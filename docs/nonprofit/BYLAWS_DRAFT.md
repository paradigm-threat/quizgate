# Bylaws — draft outline

**Not legal advice.** Adopt at the organizational board meeting; store signed copy with corporate records.

## Article I — Name and offices
Legal name; principal office; registered agent reference.

## Article II — Purpose
Restate educational purpose aligned with Articles; reference QuizGate educational program and curriculum/guardrails mission. Explicitly **exclude** commercial products of Clevertree LLC (e.g. MyProblem) from corporate purpose.

## Article III — Board of Directors
- Number: at least **3**; prefer odd number.
- Independence: aim for a majority not employed by Clevertree LLC / not receiving material related-party compensation.
- Terms, election, removal, vacancies, meetings, quorum, voting.
- Committees (optional): audit/finance, curriculum advisory (non-fiduciary OK).

## Article IV — Officers
Chair/President, Secretary, Treasurer (may be combined where GA law allows); duties; no unchecked single-signer for large transfers without board policy.

## Article V — Conflicts and compensation
Adopt written **Conflict of Interest** policy; annual disclosure. Compensation only if reasonable, documented, and approved by disinterested directors.

## Article VI — Related-party transactions
Any license, assignment, or contract with Clevertree LLC (including QuizGate IP) requires board approval by disinterested directors and written agreement.

## Article VII — Finances
Fiscal year; books; bank accounts **separate from Clevertree LLC**; no co-mingling. Donations held for educational purposes described in the mission.

## Article VIII — Records and transparency
Minutes, Articles, bylaws, Form 990/990-N when applicable; public educational materials encouraged.

## Article IX — Amendment
Board vote threshold to amend bylaws (must remain 501(c)(3)-compatible).

## Article X — Dissolution
Assets to another 501(c)(3) or government per Articles.
