# QuizGate IP license / assignment — draft term sheet

**Not legal advice.** Clevertree LLC (or current rights holders) → new nonprofit. Prefer a written **exclusive license for educational use** or **assignment** once the nonprofit exists. Board of both sides (or LLC manager + nonprofit board) should approve.

## Parties
- **Licensor:** Clevertree LLC (Georgia)  
- **Licensee:** [Nonprofit legal name], Inc.

## Subject matter
- QuizGate software (source and binaries), package id `net.clevertree.quizgate`  
- Trademarks / names “QuizGate” as used for the education app  
- Store listing content, domain names used for QuizGate (`quizgate.app` and related) as agreed  
- Curriculum packs and pedagogy materials designated as QuizGate education content  

## Grant (choose one with counsel)
- **A. Assignment** — LLC assigns copyright/trademark in the Subject to the nonprofit.  
- **B. Exclusive educational license** — LLC retains bare legal title; nonprofit has exclusive worldwide right to use, modify, distribute, and sublicense for educational / 501(c)(3) purposes; forever-free end-user access required.

## Key terms
- **Royalty:** $0 or nominal (document fair dealing; avoid private inurement).  
- **Forever free:** Licensee shall not put ads, IAP, or paywalls on QuizGate end-user apps.  
- **Improvements:** Clarify ownership of nonprofit-funded curriculum expansion.  
- **Play Console:** Permit listing under LLC until transfer; then transfer or publish under nonprofit account.  
- **MyProblem / other LLC products:** Explicitly **out of scope**.  
- **Term:** Perpetual while nonprofit remains educational / tax-exempt-seeking; survival of free-user covenants.  
- **Termination:** Only for material breach after cure; user rights to already-distributed free software preserved as agreed.

- [ ] Exact schedules (repos, domains, marks) attached
