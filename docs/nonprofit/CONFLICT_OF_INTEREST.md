# Conflict of Interest Policy — draft

**Not legal advice.** Adapt from IRS sample COI for 501(c)(3) organizations; each director signs annually.

## 1. Purpose
Protect the nonprofit’s interest when contemplating a transaction that might benefit a director, officer, or substantial contributor’s private interest — especially dealings with **Clevertree LLC** or affiliates.

## 2. Definitions
**Interested person:** director, officer, or member of a committee with board authority who has a direct or indirect financial interest (ownership, compensation, or family interest) in an entity party to a proposed transaction.

## 3. Disclosure
Interested persons must disclose the existence and nature of the financial interest to the board **before** discussion/vote.

## 4. Procedures
- Leave the room for deliberation/vote when appropriate.  
- Disinterested board determines whether a conflict exists.  
- If a conflict exists: explore alternatives; approve only if fair, reasonable, and in the nonprofit’s best interest (document comparability where compensation is involved).  

## 5. Annual statements
Each director/officer signs yearly: received copy of policy; read and understands; agrees to comply; affirms tax-exempt educational purpose.

## 6. Records
Minutes note attendees, disclosures, votes, and alternatives considered.
